{{ group }} asks that members not engage in cross talk. Examples of cross talk include commenting on a person’s share, interrupting someone who is sharing, and giving advice to a person who has already shared. We ask that members not cross talk, either out loud or by messaging everyone in the chat.

Today we’re going to use the Topicificator to help guide our sharing.

There are four simple ways to use it, and you can choose whatever feels right for you.

First, you can ask for a Random Topic, and the Topicificator will choose something for you.

Second, you can Pick a Number. Each number goes with a topic, and we’ll share on that.

Third, you can choose a topic that was Previously Shared, if something you heard really resonated with you and you’d like to build on it.

And fourth, if none of that feels right, you’re always welcome to just do a Check-In: share where you’re at today, what’s going on, or how you’re feeling.

There’s no right or wrong way to use it. It’s just a tool to help get us talking and keep the focus on experience, strength and hope.

Meeting Security Guidelines (reviewed and approved by the group conscience on (date))

  1. Preventing Zoom bombing:

    1. The co-host manages the waiting room.

      1. Everyone will be let in to the meeting; we will not refuse anyone (Tradition 3).

      2. The waiting room is a buffer, so Zoom bombers can’t all come in at once.

    2. (If your group agreed) Ask for a camera to check that the attendee is a person and not a robot. Once you’ve seen them, they can turn their camera off.

    3. Turning off “Allow participants to unmute themselves” is up to the host and co-host. If you do, attendees raise their hand to ask to be unmuted. Phone users raise their hand by pressing *9.

    4. (Renaming, locking the meeting and the chat: what your group conscience decided.) You may lock the meeting or turn off the chat during a Zoom bombing to take control of the meeting.

  2. If a Zoom bombing happens:

    1. Right-click the person’s picture and choose “Put in waiting room”, “Remove” or “Report”.

    2. These are also under the ••• on the person in the Participants panel.
