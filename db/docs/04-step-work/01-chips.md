Your **Chips** page (Chips in the sidebar) shows every chip you've earned, as 3D chips you can spin.

## Kinds of chips

- **Milestone chips** from your [sobriety date](/docs/getting-started/sobriety-date): 24 hours, 30, 60 and 90 days, 6 and 9 months, 18 months and every year.
- **Streaks** for keeping up a habit, like writing your nightly 10th step: 7, 30, 90, 180 and 365 days in a row.
- **Meeting Maker** chips for joining meetings on time with the Join button: 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100 meetings, then every 100.
- **Given chips** that a host of one of your groups [gave you](/docs/step-work/give-a-chip). These can carry your group's own design.

## Look at a chip

Tap a chip to open it over the whole screen. Drag to turn it over and read the back.

## Favorites on your page

Pick up to five favorite chips to show on [your page](/docs/community/your-page) as a block you can move. It's off until you turn it on.

## Share a chip

Choose **Share to my timeline** on a chip to post it for your followers.

> [!PRIVACY] Chips only show where you put them. Nobody sees your chips page but you.
