Reminders come as a notification (and to your phone, if you've [turned notifications on](/docs/getting-started/notifications)).

## Set them up

- **Gratitude** and **10th step**: switch the reminder on at the top of each page and pick a time.
- **Worksheets**: in a worksheet's ••• menu, choose the days and time to be reminded to keep going.
- All of them are also in **Settings > Reminders**.

## How they behave

- If you've already written today's, there's no reminder.
- Turning one on after today's time starts tomorrow.
- Reminders follow your time zone, which your browser tells us when you set them.
