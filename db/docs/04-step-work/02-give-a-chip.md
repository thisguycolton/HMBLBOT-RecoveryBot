Hosts can give a chip to someone celebrating, from their group's folder in the sidebar (**Give a chip**), or with **Give a chip** at the top of the Chips page.

![Give a chip](/docs-images/step-work/give-a-chip.webp)

## Give one

1. Open **Give a chip**.
2. In **To**, start typing a member's name, or enter someone's email.
3. Pick the **Chip** (30 days, 1 year…), the number of years for year chips, and the day it was **Celebrated on**.
4. Add a note if you like ("Keep coming back!").
5. Choose **Give the chip**.

The chip on the right shows how it will look in your group's design. Drag to spin it.

Someone with an account gets it right away, with a notification. Someone without one gets an email with a link to claim it when they sign up.

## Example: Saturday's chips

At the Saturday Speaker Meeting, three people celebrate. After the meeting Sam gives:

- **Taylor B.**: 60 days.
- **Jordan K.**: 90 days, with a note: "Ninety days! Proud of you."

Morgan gave **Alex P.** a 1 year chip last month. All of them show under **Given**, newest first, with who gave each one. **Take back** undoes a chip given by mistake.

## Your group's design

Group admins design the chips their group gives in **Settings > Chips**: the text around the top and bottom, the words on the back, what's in the middle (circle and triangle, an illustration, the group's logo printed in color or raised in metal, or just the number), the finish, and how years are written (Roman numerals or numbers). The preview updates as you go. Earned chips stay plain; only chips your hosts give carry the group's look.

![Designing the group's chips](/docs-images/step-work/chip-design.webp)

> [!NOTE] To keep inboxes safe, each person can send up to 25 chip emails a day.
