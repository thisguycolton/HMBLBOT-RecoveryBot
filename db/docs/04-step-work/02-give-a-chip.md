Hosts can give a chip to someone celebrating, from their group's folder in the sidebar (**Give a chip**).

## Give one

1. Open **Give a chip** in your group's folder.
2. In **To**, start typing a member's name, or enter someone's email.
3. Pick the chip (30 days, 1 year…) and add a note if you like.
4. Choose **Give**.

Someone with an account gets it right away, with a notification. Someone without one gets an email with a link to claim it when they sign up.

## Your group's design

Group admins can design the chips their group gives: colors, the group's logo printed in color or raised in metal, and the text around the edge. Earned chips stay plain; only chips your hosts give carry the group's look.

> [!NOTE] To keep inboxes safe, each person can send up to 25 chip emails a day.
