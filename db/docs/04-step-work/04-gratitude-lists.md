**Gratitude** (in the sidebar) is a fresh list each day.

## Write today's list

1. Open **Gratitude**.
2. Add what you're grateful for, one line at a time.
3. Look back at earlier days from the calendar.

Writing one each day builds a streak, and streaks earn [chips](/docs/step-work/chips).

## Reminder and sharing

- Turn on the **reminder** and pick a time of day.
- **Share** copies your list as text, with a link for the reader to start their own. The link says nothing about you.
- **Send to my sponsor** sends it on RecoveryBot.
