**Gratitude** (in the sidebar) is a fresh list each day.

![Today's gratitude list](/docs-images/step-work/gratitude.webp)

## Write today's list

1. Open **Gratitude**.
2. Add what you're grateful for, one thing on each line. **Another** adds a line.
3. Choose **Save list**.
4. Look back at earlier days with the arrows, or under **Past days**.

Writing one each day builds a streak ("3 days in a row"), and streaks earn [chips](/docs/step-work/chips).

## Example

Sam's list for today:

1. My sponsor picked up on the first ring
2. Coffee with Quinn after the meeting
3. Sleeping through the night
4. The dog

Yesterday's was shorter: "A quiet morning, Thursday's meeting, my sister called." Big or small, it all counts.

## Reminder and sharing

- Turn on **Remind me to write my gratitude list** and pick a time of day.
- **Share** copies your list as text, with a link for the reader to start their own. The link says nothing about you.
- **Send to my sponsor** sends it on RecoveryBot.

> [!PRIVACY] Only you can ever read your lists here, unless you send one to your sponsor (Share), and they go when you delete your account.
