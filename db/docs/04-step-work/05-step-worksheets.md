**Steps** (in the sidebar) has worksheets for every step: Step 1 questions, several ways to do a 4th step inventory, the Drop the Rock workbook for steps 6 and 7, an 8th step list and 9th step tracker, and more.

![The Steps page](/docs-images/step-work/steps.webp)

The top of the page also shows what **Needs your attention** (tonight's 10th step, today's gratitude list) and your latest chips.

## Start a worksheet

1. Open **Steps** and choose a step tile.
2. Pick a worksheet. Steps with more than one (like Step 4) show what makes each different.
3. Choose **Start**.

## Working through it

A worksheet opens on its **Welcome** page: the introduction, a **reading list** to tick off as you read, and **Done so far**, so you can pick up where you left off.

![A Step 1 worksheet's Welcome page](/docs-images/step-work/worksheet.webp)

- It goes **one page at a time**. **Next** and **Back** are at the bottom; the **Pages** rail on the left (or the bar on a phone) jumps to any page.
- Nothing is required. Skip anything and come back later.
- It saves as you type ("Saved" at the top right).
- The **Review** page at the end shows what you wrote, then **Mark done**, **Send to my sponsor** or **Print**.

Prefer one long page? Choose **All on one page** in the **•••** menu.

## Example: Jordan's Step 1

Jordan started **Powerlessness and unmanageability** for Step 1. The Welcome page shows Jordan has read 2 of the 3 readings (The Doctor's Opinion and Chapter 3, with **Open** links straight to them in the Library) and is 3 of 4 pages in:

- **Times I couldn't control it once I started**: 2 rows, like "My cousin's wedding: meant to have two glasses of wine, then switch to soda. Left before the toasts."
- **Ways I tried to control or stop**: 4 written ("Only beer", "Only on weekends"…).
- **How my life became unmanageable**: 2 of 5 answered.

**Pick up where I left off** goes straight to the next empty page. Tables fill in **A row at a time** or **A column at a time**:

![Filling in a table, a row at a time](/docs-images/step-work/worksheet-page.webp)

## Make it your own

Choose **Edit** in the ••• menu to open the **HumbleBot Editor**: add, remove and reorder sections, change questions, add readings, and set the pages. **Build your own** on any step tile starts from blank.

## Print and share

- **Print** or **Print blank**.
- **Share a link** with a password, for someone to read it (they need both).
- **Share a blank copy** with your sponsees or your group. They get the worksheet, never your answers.

> [!PRIVACY] Your answers are encrypted where they're stored, and only you can read them unless you send or share them.
