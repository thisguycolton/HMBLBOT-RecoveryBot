**10th step** (in the sidebar) is a nightly inventory: a few questions about your day, in the spirit of "When we retire at night, we constructively review our day."

![Tonight's 10th step](/docs-images/step-work/inventory.webp)

## Write tonight's

1. Open **10th step**. Tonight's page is ready under **Today**.
2. Answer each question with **Yes** or **No**, and add a note if you like. It saves as you type.
3. Come back to any night with the arrows, or from **Past days** below.

The bar above the questions counts your streak ("1 night in a row") and your best.

## Example

Last night Jordan answered:

| Question | Answer | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Was I resentful? | Yes | Got short with a driver in traffic. Let it go by lunch. |
| Was I selfish? | No | |
| Was I afraid? | Yes | Worried about the job interview. |
| Was I kind and loving toward all? | Yes | Called Casey to check in. |
| What could I have done better? | | Pause before I answer emails. |

And at the end: "A good day overall. Grateful."

## Your own questions

The questions start as the ones on page 86 of the Big Book. Choose **Edit questions** to change, add or remove questions, or pick another set: the four from page 84 (resentment, selfishness, dishonesty, fear) or a Twelve and Twelve balance sheet. Each night keeps the questions it was written with.

## A reminder

Turn on **Remind me to do my 10th step** at the top and pick a time. If you've already written tonight's, it doesn't bother you.

![The 10th step on a phone](/docs-images/step-work/inventory-phone.webp)

## Share it

**Share** makes plain text to copy, text or email, with the option to leave your notes out. **Send to my sponsor** sends it to them on RecoveryBot. See [Sponsorship](/docs/step-work/sponsorship).

> [!PRIVACY] Your inventories are yours alone. They're only seen by someone you send one to.
