**Sponsorship** (in the sidebar) connects sponsors and sponsees on RecoveryBot. It doesn't replace the phone call; it makes passing along your work easier.

## Ask someone to be your sponsor

1. Open **Sponsorship** and choose **Ask a sponsor**.
2. Enter their username, or send them an **invite link** (there's a QR code to show in person).
3. When they accept, you're connected. If they decline, it's quiet: nobody is told.

## Send your work

From a 10th step, a gratitude list or a worksheet, choose **Send to my sponsor**. They get a copy as it is when you send it. They can leave **one short note** back on each thing you send.

You can have a worksheet send itself when you mark it done, or set a reminder to keep working on it.

## For sponsors

- See what each sponsee sent, and which **step** they're on (either of you can set it).
- **Nudge** a sponsee who's gone quiet (once a day at most).
- **Share a blank copy** of a worksheet you like to a sponsee.

## Available to sponsor

Turn on **Available to sponsor** to show a green check next to your name in your groups, with who you'll sponsor. Only people in your groups see it. Your account needs to be a week old first.

## Ending a sponsorship

Either of you can end it at any time, quietly. Everything you sent disappears from your sponsor's view.
