RecoveryBot doesn't need an app store. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen like any other app. On iPhone and iPad this is also what lets it send you notifications.

> [!TIP] The [Get the app](/install) page walks you through it for the device you're on, with tabs for iPhone & iPad, Android and computers.

![The Get the app page on an iPhone](/docs-images/getting-started/install-phone.webp)

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open RecoveryBot in **Safari** (on iOS 16.4 or later, Chrome and Edge work too).
2. Tap the **Share** button: the square with an arrow, at the bottom of the screen on iPhone or at the top on iPad. If you only see **•••**, tap that first, then **Share**.
3. Scroll down the list and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Leave **Open as Web App** on if you see it, and tap **Add**.
5. Open RecoveryBot from the new icon and sign in.

## Android

1. Open RecoveryBot in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install app** when it's offered, or open the **⋮** menu and choose **Add to Home screen**.

## Computers

In Chrome or Edge, choose the install icon at the right of the address bar. On a Mac with Safari, choose **File > Add to Dock**.

## After you install

- Turn on [notifications](/docs/getting-started/notifications) for this device.
- Pull down at the top of any page to refresh it.
- Printing from the iPhone home-screen app opens the page in Safari first, because home-screen apps can't print on their own.
- Until you install, the home feed shows a small **Add RecoveryBot to your home screen** card. Close it with ✕ if you'd rather not.
