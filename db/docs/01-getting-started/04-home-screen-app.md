RecoveryBot doesn't need an app store. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen like any other app. On iPhone and iPad this is also what lets it send you notifications.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open RecoveryBot in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open RecoveryBot from the new icon and sign in.

## Android

1. Open RecoveryBot in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install app** when it's offered, or open the **⋮** menu and choose **Add to Home screen**.

## Computers

In Chrome or Edge, choose the install icon at the right of the address bar. On a Mac with Safari, choose **File > Add to Dock**.

> [!TIP] The [Get the app](/install) page walks you through it for the device you're on.

## After you install

- Turn on [notifications](/docs/getting-started/notifications) for this device.
- Pull down at the top of any page to refresh it.
- Printing from the iPhone home-screen app opens the page in Safari first, because home-screen apps can't print on their own.
