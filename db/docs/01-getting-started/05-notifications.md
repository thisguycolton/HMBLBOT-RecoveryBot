Everything that lands in the bell can also be sent to your devices as a notification.

## Turn them on

1. Open **Settings** from your account menu and go to **Notifications on this device** (it's also on the [Get the app](/install) page).
2. Turn the switch on and allow notifications when your browser asks.
3. Choose **Send a test** to make sure one arrives.

On iPhone and iPad, notifications only work from the [home-screen app](/docs/getting-started/home-screen-app).

## Pick what you get

Notifications come in kinds you can turn on and off: mentions and replies, chips, hosting reminders, recovery reminders, sponsorship, group news and more. Your choices are kept per account; each device just needs to be switched on once.

## Reminders

Gratitude, 10th step and hosting reminders are switched on separately, in **Settings > Reminders** and **Hosting**. Nothing is sent until you turn one on. See [Reminders](/docs/step-work/reminders).

![Reminder switches in Settings](/docs-images/getting-started/reminders-settings.webp)

## Other devices

The same settings list your other devices that get notifications. Remove one you no longer use. Logging out on a device stops its notifications too.

> [!NOTE] Only real activity sends notifications. We never send marketing.
