Plenty of RecoveryBot works without an account: finding a meeting, the Topicificator, the Service Readings and the Library. An account lets you keep your own things (highlights, chips, step work), join groups and get notifications.

## Sign up

1. Choose **Sign up** in the top right corner.
2. Enter your name, email and a password. Your name can be just a first name and last initial.
3. Tick **I'm not a robot**. It's a small puzzle your device solves by itself; you only wait a moment.
4. Open the email we send you and choose **Confirm my account**. The link works for 3 days.

> [!TIP] No email? Check your spam folder, then ask for a new one at **Didn't get the email?** on the sign-in page.

## Your username

Every account gets a username (like `@colton-h`) made from your name. People use it to find you, mention you and ask you to sponsor them. Change it any time under **Settings**.

## Change your email or password

Open your account menu (your picture, top right) and choose **Settings**. A new email address starts working once you confirm it from the email we send there.

## Delete your account

In **Settings**, at the bottom, choose **Delete my account** and enter your password. Your notes, highlights, step work and the rest go with it. If you've published readings to the Reading Archive, we'll ask you to [contact us](/contact) first so they can be handed over or removed properly.

> [!PRIVACY] We never sell or share your email. See the [privacy policy](/privacy) for everything we keep and why.
