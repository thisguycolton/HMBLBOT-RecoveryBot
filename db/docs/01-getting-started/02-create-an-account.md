Plenty of RecoveryBot works without an account: finding a meeting, the Topicificator, the Service Readings and the Library. An account lets you keep your own things (highlights, chips, step work), join groups and get notifications.

## Sign up

1. Choose **Sign up** in the top right corner.
2. Enter your name, email and a password (at least 8 characters). Your name can be just a first name and last initial, like **Jordan K.**
3. Tick **I'm not a robot**. It's a small puzzle your device solves by itself; you only wait a moment.
4. Choose **Sign up**, then open the email we send you and choose **Confirm my account**. The link works for 3 days.

![The sign-up form](/docs-images/getting-started/sign-up.webp)

> [!TIP] No email? Check your spam folder, then ask for a new one at **Didn't get the email?** on the sign-in page.

## Your username

Every account gets a username made from your name. Jordan K. becomes `@jordan-k`; if that's taken, a number is added. People use it to find you, mention you and ask you to sponsor them. Change it any time under **Settings**: lowercase letters, numbers and dashes.

## Your profile

**Settings** opens on your profile: your picture, name, username, a short **About you** (up to 280 characters) and your [sobriety date](/docs/getting-started/sobriety-date). Choose **Save profile** when you're done.

![The profile card in Settings](/docs-images/getting-started/profile-settings.webp)

## Change your email or password

Open your account menu (your picture, top right) and choose **Settings**, then scroll to **Account**. Enter your current password to change either one. A new email address starts working once you confirm it from the email we send there.

## Delete your account

In **Settings**, at the bottom, choose **Delete account…** and enter your password. Your profile, notes, highlights, follows, RSVPs and group memberships go with it. Records your groups keep, like minutes or a document you edited, stay with the group. If you've published readings to the Reading Archive, we'll ask you to [contact us](/contact) first so they can be handed over or removed properly.

> [!PRIVACY] We never sell or share your email. See the [privacy policy](/privacy) for everything we keep and why.
