Your sobriety date drives your day count, your milestone celebrations and your [chips](/docs/step-work/chips).

## Set it

1. Open **Settings** from your account menu.
2. Enter your date under **Sobriety date**. RecoveryBot shows how long that is right next to it (for example, "4 years sober").
3. Choose whether to **Show it to the people in my groups**.
4. Choose **Save profile**.

![Sobriety date in your profile settings](/docs-images/getting-started/profile-settings.webp)

You can change it at any time, and no one checks it. We don't police anyone's sobriety. The ✕ next to the date clears it.

## What you'll see

- **Your day count** when you sign in, and at the top of Today: "1,461 days, happy 4 years!"
- **Milestones**: 24 hours, 30, 60 and 90 days, 6 and 9 months, 18 months and every year get a celebration (with confetti) on the day.
- **Chips** for each milestone, ready on your [Chips page](/docs/step-work/chips), and a preview of the next one ("5 years, in 365 days").
- If you choose to show it, groups you're in can list your sobriety birthday so they can celebrate with you, and your page shows "Sober 4 years".

![Chips earned from a sobriety date](/docs-images/step-work/chips.webp)

> [!PRIVACY] Your sobriety date only shows where you choose to share it.
