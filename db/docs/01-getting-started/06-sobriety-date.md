Your sobriety date drives your day count, your milestone celebrations and your [chips](/docs/step-work/chips).

## Set it

1. Open **Settings** from your account menu.
2. Enter your sobriety date and save.

You can change it at any time, and no one checks it. We don't police anyone's sobriety.

## What you'll see

- **Your day count** when you sign in, and at the top of Today.
- **Milestones**: 24 hours, 30, 60 and 90 days, 6 and 9 months, 18 months and every year get a celebration (with confetti) on the day.
- **Chips** for each milestone, ready on your [Chips page](/docs/step-work/chips).
- If you choose to show it, groups you're in can list your sobriety birthday so they can celebrate with you.

> [!PRIVACY] Your sobriety date only shows where you choose to share it.
