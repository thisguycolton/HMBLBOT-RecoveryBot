Every page sits in the same frame: a sidebar on the left, a top bar with the search box, and the page itself in the middle (we call it **the stage**).

![The frame on a computer: sidebar, top bar and the stage](/docs-images/getting-started/frame.webp)

## The sidebar

The sidebar is a short list. Most items open a **sub menu** of their pages: choose one and the list folds down to its icons while the sub menu slides in beside it. The back arrow, the same icon again, or **Esc** closes it. On a page that belongs to a sub menu, that sub menu is already open, so you can see where you are.

- **Home**: your home feed.
- **Recovery**: Chips, Steps, 10th step, Gratitude and Sponsorship.
- **Read**: the Library, Readings, My readings and your Highlights.
- **Meetings**: Find a meeting, Hosting, the Topicificator, Service Readings and the Host-burger Helper.
- **Your groups**: each group's logo opens its pages: Home, Meetings, Chat, Events, Conscience, Members and HostDeck. If you hold a role in a group (admin, record keeper, host coordinator, treasurer), you'll also see its **Dashboard**.
- At the bottom: **Help & docs** (these pages), **Extras**, and your picture for **My page**.

Numbers tell you something is waiting: **Hosting 1** means one meeting needs a sub, and a group's **Chat** shows unread messages. While the words are hidden, a dot on the icon says the same. Choose **Collapse** at the bottom to keep the sidebar as icons; sub menus then open as a card over the page.

## Search

The search box at the top finds groups, people, readings, chapters of books, service readings, hashtags and pages of these docs. Press **/** anywhere to jump to it, use the arrow keys to move, and Enter to open.

For example, typing **lantern** finds the Lantern Street Group, its welcome reading and the readings its members wrote:

## Today

**Today** (the calendar button on phones, the right column of your home feed on a computer) greets you with your day count and lists what needs you this week: meetings you're hosting, who is filling in for whom, and anything your roles ask of you. Clear an item with its ✕ when you've handled it.

In the frame picture above, Sam's Today says:

- **Your day count**: 1,461 days, happy 4 years.
- **Needs your attention**: no host yet for the Thursday meeting on Nov 26 (Sam is a host coordinator), and 1 suggested agenda item (Sam is a record keeper).
- **Filling in**: Sam is covering for Alex P. as co-host on Saturday.
- **Next meetings**, each with a **Join** button and its passcode.

## The bell

The bell in the top bar collects your notifications: mentions, replies, chips you've been given, host reminders and more. Unread ones have a dot; **Mark all read** clears them. Opening the page a notification points to marks it read. To get them on your phone too, see [Notifications](/docs/getting-started/notifications).

![The bell, open](/docs-images/getting-started/bell.webp)

## On a phone

A floating bar at the bottom holds: **Menu**, **Home**, the **robot** (quick actions like writing a note or tonight's 10th step), **Today**, and the page's **side panel** when it has one. Pull down at the top of a page to refresh it.

![The home feed on a phone](/docs-images/getting-started/home-phone.webp)

**Menu** opens the whole sidebar as a sheet, with your account, search, Donate, the theme and the bell at the bottom. Tap an item to slide into its sub menu, and the back arrow to come out again:
