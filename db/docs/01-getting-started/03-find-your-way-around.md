Every page sits in the same frame: a sidebar on the left, a top bar with the search box, and the page itself in the middle (we call it **the stage**).

## The sidebar

- **You**: your home feed, your page, highlights, readings, chips, 10th step, gratitude, steps and sponsorship.
- **Your groups**: a folder for each group you're in, with its Home, Meetings, Chat, Events, Conscience, Members and HostDeck pages. If you hold a role in a group (admin, record keeper, host coordinator, treasurer), you'll also see its **Dashboard**.
- **Explore**: Find a meeting, the Library, Readings, the Topicificator, Service Readings, the Host-burger Helper and these docs.

On a phone the sidebar is behind the menu button at the bottom left.

## Search

The search box at the top finds groups, people, readings, chapters of books, service readings, hashtags and pages of these docs. Press <kbd>/</kbd> anywhere to jump to it, use the arrow keys to move, and Enter to open.

## Today

**Today** (the calendar button on phones, the right column of your home feed on a computer) greets you with your day count and lists what needs you this week: meetings you're hosting, who is filling in for whom, and anything your roles ask of you. Clear an item when you've handled it.

## The bell

The bell in the top bar collects your notifications: mentions, replies, chips you've been given, host reminders and more. Opening the page a notification points to marks it read. To get them on your phone too, see [Notifications](/docs/getting-started/notifications).

## On a phone

A floating bar at the bottom holds: **Menu**, **Home**, the **robot** (quick actions like writing a note or tonight's 10th step), **Today**, and the page's **side panel** when it has one. Pull down at the top of a page to refresh it.
