RecoveryBot is a free website (and home-screen app) with tools for people in twelve-step recovery and for the groups that hold meetings. It started as a way to make one home group's online meetings run a little smoother, and grew from there.

## What you can do here

- **Find a meeting**, online or in person, and join with one tap. See [Find a meeting](/docs/meetings/find-a-meeting).
- **Run a meeting**: draw a topic with the [Topicificator](/docs/meetings/topicificator), read the [Service Readings](/docs/meetings/service-readings) aloud, or follow your group's script in the [Hosting Command Center](/docs/group-tools/hosting-command-center).
- **Read the literature** in the [Library](/docs/library/reading-the-books), with your own highlights and notes.
- **Work your program**: [chips](/docs/step-work/chips) for your milestones, a [nightly 10th step](/docs/step-work/nightly-10th-step), [gratitude lists](/docs/step-work/gratitude-lists), [step worksheets](/docs/step-work/step-worksheets) and [sponsorship](/docs/step-work/sponsorship).
- **Keep your group organized**: meetings and host schedules, chat, events, business meetings, decisions, documents and treasury, all in one place. Start with [Start or join a group](/docs/group-tools/start-or-join-a-group).

## What it isn't

RecoveryBot isn't part of Alcoholics Anonymous or any other fellowship, and it isn't medical or professional advice. It's a set of tools made by people in recovery, for people in recovery.

> [!PRIVACY] Your step work, inventories and gratitude lists are yours. Nobody else sees them unless you send them to someone. Read [Privacy and anonymity](/docs/getting-started/privacy-and-anonymity) for the details.
