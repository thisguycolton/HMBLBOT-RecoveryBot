**The Acid Quest** turns the Topicificator into a 16-bit group journey. Your group travels a map together; each stop is a topic to talk about, with choices that shape the road ahead.

![The Acid Quest's start screen](/docs-images/extras/acid-quest.webp)

## Play

1. Open **The Acid Quest** from the sidebar's extras.
2. Start a session and share the **join code** with the people playing with you.
3. Move across the map. At each stop, read the topic and share around the room.
4. Decide together how much risk to take, spend resources, and open new paths.

The storyteller keeps the tale going between topics. Your progress is saved, so you can pick it back up next meeting.

> [!TIP] It works best on a shared screen, with one person driving and everyone deciding.
