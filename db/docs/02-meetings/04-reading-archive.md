The **Reading Archive** keeps readings that hosts have published: the passage they read and the topic it led to.

![The Reading Archive](/docs-images/meetings/reading-archive.webp)

## Browse

1. Open **Readings** from the sidebar.
2. Search by title or source in the box at the top right, or pick a theme under **Browse by theme** (tags like gratitude, help, newcomers).
3. Open a reading to read it, see its tags, and share it.

## A reading, opened

Each reading shows the meeting it was read at, its tags, and buttons for the **Host**, the **Group**, **Copy for chat** and **Share**. Below the text are example topics for sharing.

![A reading from the archive](/docs-images/meetings/reading.webp)

For example, "On asking for help" was read at the Lantern Street Thursday Night Step Meeting and is tagged **help** and **newcomers**. The host ended it with tonight's topic: "the first time we asked for help, and what made it possible."

## Add your own

Signed in, you can write readings of your own and publish them to the archive. See [My readings](/docs/library/my-readings).

## Polls on readings

A reading can carry a [poll](/docs/meetings/polls) (**Add a poll** under the reading), so people can answer a question about the topic during the meeting.

> [!NOTE] Shared links show the reading's title and a picture in Messages and other apps, but never its text.
