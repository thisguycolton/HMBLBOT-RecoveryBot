**Find a meeting** lists online meetings from AA Intergroup, in-person meetings from local intergroups, and the meetings of groups on RecoveryBot. No account needed.

![Find a meeting on a computer](/docs-images/meetings/find-a-meeting.webp)

## Search

1. Open **Find a meeting** from the sidebar (Explore).
2. Type a meeting or group name in the box, or paste a Zoom link or meeting ID to find which meeting it belongs to.
3. Pick when: **Coming week** (the default), **Live now**, **Today**, **Tomorrow**, or **Pick a date**.
4. Narrow it down with the filters: **Time of day**, **Where** (online or in person), **Location** (a city, region or time zone) and **Type** (open, closed, speaker, Big Book…).
5. Switch between **Grid** and **List** at the top right of the results.

Times always show in **your** time zone, whatever zone the meeting is in. The line under the title says which one ("Times are in your time zone (MST)").

## Example: a Monday morning meeting

Say Jordan wants a meeting before work on Monday:

1. Choose **Pick a date** and pick Monday.
2. Set **Time of day** to mornings and **Where** to Online.
3. The **List** view shows the Sunrise Big Book Study at 6:30 AM and the Lunch Bunch at noon, each with its passcode and type tags.

![The same search as a list](/docs-images/meetings/find-a-meeting-list.webp)

## Join

- Meetings live now say **Live now**; others count down to their next start ("in 1 day 10h").
- **Join** opens the meeting link (Zoom, Google Meet…). The passcode, when there is one, shows next to it with a copy button.
- In-person meetings show the address and a small map. **Open in Maps** opens your phone's maps app.
- The share button on a card copies a link to that meeting.

![Find a meeting on a phone](/docs-images/meetings/find-a-meeting-phone.webp)

> [!TIP] Your filters stay in the page address, so you can bookmark or share a search like "Tonight, women's meetings".

## A group's own page

Choose a meeting's group to see its page: every meeting it holds, its next meeting, and anything the group shares publicly (events, readings, documents). Signed in, you can **Ask to join**. See [Start or join a group](/docs/group-tools/start-or-join-a-group).

## Something's wrong in a listing?

Online listings come from AA Intergroup and refresh about once a week. If your group is on RecoveryBot, its admins can correct its meetings in [Group settings](/docs/group-tools/group-settings). Otherwise, [let us know](/contact).
