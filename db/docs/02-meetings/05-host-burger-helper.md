The **Host-burger Helper** is a scratch pad for whoever is hosting tonight. It keeps the little things in one place so you don't juggle sticky notes.

## What's on it

- **Service roles**: who's the co-host, who's timing, who's reading How It Works and the closing reading.
- **Milestones**: people celebrating time tonight, by screen name. Drag them into the order you'll call them.
- **Timer** and **closing reading** reminders.

## How to use it

1. Open **Host-burger Helper** from the sidebar before the meeting.
2. Fill in the roles and add anyone celebrating.
3. Keep it open beside Zoom while you host.

> [!TIP] If your group is on RecoveryBot, the [Hosting Command Center](/docs/group-tools/hosting-command-center) does all this and follows your group's own script.
