**Polls** ask one question with a few answers. Share the short link (or show the QR code on screen) and results update live as people vote.

## Make a poll

1. Open **Polls** (signed in) and choose **New poll**.
2. Write the question and the answers.
3. Choose the settings:
   - **Who can vote**: anyone with the link, people signed in, or members of a group.
   - **When results show**: always, after you vote, or after the poll closes.
   - **Who sees who voted**: only the poll's makers, members, or everyone.
   - **Guest names**: optional, required or off for people without an account.
4. Choose **Create** and share the link (`/p/…`) or the QR code.

## During a meeting

- Add a poll to a [reading](/docs/meetings/reading-archive), and it shows right under the reading. Opened for screen sharing, the QR code shows big and voters' names stay hidden.
- Record keepers can **Take a poll on this** for a motion in [Decisions](/docs/group-tools/decisions-and-roberts-rules). When it closes, **Use these counts** fills in the motion's vote.

## Close a poll

Open it and choose **Close poll**. Closed polls keep their results.
