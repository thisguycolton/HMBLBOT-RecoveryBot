**Polls** ask one question with a few answers. Share the short link (or show the QR code on screen) and results update live as people vote.

![A poll with live results](/docs-images/meetings/poll.webp)

## Make a poll

1. Open **Polls** (signed in) and choose **New poll**.
2. Write the question, anything more about it, and the answers (**Add an answer** for more). Tick **People can pick more than one** if they should.
3. Choose whose poll it is: just yours, or one of your groups'.
4. Choose the settings:
   - **Who can vote**: anyone with the link, people signed in, or members of a group.
   - **Show results**: always, after they vote, or when it closes.
   - **Who sees who voted for what**: only the people running it, members, or everyone.
   - **Guest names**: optional, required or off for people without an account.
   - **Closes**: a date and time, or leave it empty to close it yourself.
5. Choose **Create** and share the link (`/p/…`) or the QR code.

![Making a new poll](/docs-images/meetings/new-poll.webp)

## Example: picking a date for the potluck

Sam asks the Lantern Street Group "Which day works best for the gratitude potluck?" with three evenings to pick from. Only members can vote, results show always, and members can see who voted for what. Seven people vote: Saturday the 21st wins with 4 votes, so Sam books the hall for Saturday. Under each answer, **Who voted for this** lists the names.

## During a meeting

- Add a poll to a [reading](/docs/meetings/reading-archive), and it shows right under the reading. Opened for screen sharing, the QR code shows big and voters' names stay hidden.
- Record keepers can **Take a poll on this** for a motion in [Decisions](/docs/group-tools/decisions-and-roberts-rules). When it closes, **Use these counts** fills in the motion's vote.

## Your polls

**Polls** lists the polls you've made, with who can vote and whether they're live.

![Your polls](/docs-images/meetings/polls.webp)

## Close a poll

Open it and choose **Close now**. Closed polls keep their results. **Edit** and **Delete** are next to it.
