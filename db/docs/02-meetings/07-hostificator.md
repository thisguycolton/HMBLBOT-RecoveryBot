The **Hostificator** is a quick vote between up to three proposals, like who will host a new meeting or which format to try.

## Run a vote

1. Open the Hostificator and choose **New**.
2. Add up to three proposals.
3. Share the link. Everyone who opens it can vote once.
4. Open **Results** to see the count.

> [!NOTE] For anything more than a quick show of hands, [Polls](/docs/meetings/polls) give you more control over who votes and who sees results.
