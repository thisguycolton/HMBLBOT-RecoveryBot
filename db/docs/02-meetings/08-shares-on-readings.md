Under every reading there's a **Shares** section: a place to share your experience, strength and hope about that reading, tied to it instead of posted on your timeline.

![Shares under a reading](/docs-images/meetings/reading-shares.webp)

## Share

1. Open a reading and scroll past the text and the topics to **Shares**.
2. Write what came up for you.
3. Under **Share as**, pick **A member** to stay anonymous, or your name. Your pick is remembered for next time, and you choose again on every share.
4. Choose **Share**.

Your share shows to others once the reading's poster approves it. Until then it says **Waiting for approval**, and only you and the people who approve it can see it. You can share or reply twice an hour.

Shares show their count on the reading's card in the [Reading Archive](/docs/meetings/reading-archive) and [My readings](/docs/library/my-readings), for people who can see them.

## Replies and reactions

Under an approved share, choose **Reply** to answer it. A reply picks **A member** or your name just like a share, and the reading's poster approves it too. Replies go one level deep.

React with **Like**, **Love** or **Hugs**. Everyone sees how many, never who.

## Anonymous shares

A share as **A member** shows a plain icon and "A member" to everyone else: other members, the reading's poster who approves it, and the group's and site's admins. Notifications about it never say who wrote it, and never include its words. It never appears on your profile or in anyone's feed.

You always see your own as **A member (you)**. In a small group, people who know you may still recognize your story, so share what you're comfortable with.

> [!PRIVACY] RecoveryBot keeps which account wrote each share, so you can edit or delete it and abuse can be stopped. No page shows it. See the [privacy policy](/privacy).

## Edit, delete or show your name

Choose **•••** on your share:

- **Edit**: change the words. Your share waits for approval again.
- **Show my name** or **Hide my name**: switch who it's from, any time.
- **Delete**: it's gone for everyone.

Deleting a share deletes its replies too. Deleting your account deletes all your shares and replies.

## Also post to my timeline

When you share under your name, tick **Also post to my timeline** to post it as a note too, with a link back to the reading. It goes to whoever sees your notes (your default in **Settings > Privacy**). Anonymous shares can't be posted to a timeline.

## Move a note here

Already posted a note about a reading, with a link to it? Choose **•••** on the note and **Move to the reading's shares**. Pick **A member** or your name. The note is deleted once it's moved, unless you untick that. Only its words move, not its pictures.

## Who sees shares

- Only signed-in members. Visitors to the reading's page never see them, and neither does the hosting screen shared in a meeting.
- On a group's reading, only that group's members and hosts.

## Approving shares

If you posted (or adopted) a reading, you approve the shares on it. You'll get a notification, "A share on ... is waiting for your approval", and see **Approve** and **Decline** on each waiting share. The group's admins and site admins can approve too, so shares don't get stuck while you're away.

- **Decline** asks for an optional reason. Only the share's writer sees the share and the reason after that.
- **Take down** (in **•••**) declines a share you already approved.
- **Turn shares off** (beside **Shares**) closes a reading to new shares.

Your own shares on your reading don't need approval.

## Readings from AA Grapevine

Shares are welcome on [Grapevine readings](/docs/meetings/reading-archive#readings-from-aa-grapevine), but please share your own experience and don't copy the story's text into a share. Grapevine owns its stories.

## Mute someone

Choose **•••** on a share and **Mute**. That person's shares and replies, named or anonymous, disappear for you on every reading. You still don't learn who wrote an anonymous one, and they aren't told. **Unmute all** sits at the bottom of Shares.

## Notifications

You're told when a share is waiting for your approval, when someone replies to your share (by the name they chose) and when someone first reacts ("Someone reacted to your share"). None of them include the words. Turn them off under **Shares on readings** in your notification settings.

## Reporting

Choose **•••** and **Report** on a share that breaks the rules. The site admins see the share and where it is, but not who wrote an anonymous one. They can still pause that person's sharing for 7 days, 30 days or for good, without learning who it is. Slurs are refused when you post, as everywhere on RecoveryBot. See [Blocking and reporting](/docs/community/blocking-and-reporting).
