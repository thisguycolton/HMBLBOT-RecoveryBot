The **Topicificator 9002** picks a topic for a discussion meeting: a question or a theme with a short reading to get people talking.

![A topic drawn in the Topicificator, with the share timer running](/docs-images/meetings/topicificator.webp)

## Draw a topic

1. Open **Topicificator** from the sidebar.
2. Press the blue **Random Topic** button (the shuffle icon) in the bar at the bottom. Don't like it? Press it again.
3. Read the topic aloud, or share your screen so everyone can read along.

The other buttons in the bar: **#** picks a topic by its number, the clock goes back to the previous topic, and the last one sets up a free share (check-in) instead of a topic. The copy button at the top left of the card copies the topic to paste into the Zoom chat.

## Example

The Thursday host presses **Random Topic** and gets:

> **Just for today**
> What's one thing you can do today that you couldn't promise to do for a lifetime?

The host reads it out, pastes it into the chat with the copy button, and starts the timer for the first share.

## The timer

Set a share time (for example 3 minutes) and start the timer for each person. It shows at the top right of the card ("02:59"), big and easy to read on a shared screen, with a gentle warning before time is up.

## Running it from a HostDeck

When your group hosts from a [HostDeck](/docs/group-tools/hostdeck), the host can open a **live Topicificator** window. Share that window in Zoom: the host draws topics and runs the timer from the deck, and the shared window follows along. A group can pick its own **topic set** (like "Lantern Street favorites") there.

> [!TIP] Topics are curated by the RecoveryBot team. Have a good one to suggest? [Send it to us](/contact).
