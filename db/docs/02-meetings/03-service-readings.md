**Service Readings** are the readings that open and close most meetings, laid out big and clean for reading aloud or sharing on screen.

## Use them

1. Open **Service Readings** from the sidebar.
2. Pick a reading. Each one shows its source.
3. Read it from your phone, or share your screen during an online meeting.

Use the theme button in the top bar for dark mode, which is easier on the eyes in a dim room.

## In your group's script

Group hosts can link these readings from their meeting script in the [Hosting Command Center](/docs/group-tools/hosting-command-center), so they open at the right moment of the meeting.
