Online meetings come from AA Intergroup by themselves. In-person meetings come from local intergroups' **Meeting Guide** feeds, imported in **Site Admin > Meeting lists**.

## Import a list

1. Choose **New meeting list** and name it after the intergroup.
2. Paste the intergroup's feed link, or upload a JSON or CSV file.
3. Check the **preview**. Possible duplicates are left unticked.
4. Choose **Import**.

Imported meetings show in Find a meeting and can be linked to groups. Nothing is ever deleted by an import; meetings that disappear from a feed are hidden.

To refresh a list, import it again.
