**Site Admin > Blocked words** manages the slur filter that checks notes, comments, chat and other things people write.

![Site Admin > Blocked words (the words themselves stay blurred until you choose Show words)](/docs-images/site-admin/blocked-words.webp)

- **Add a word** to block it, with how strict the match is.
- **Allow** a word that's being caught by mistake (a place name, for example).
- **Try it** checks a sentence against the rules before you save.

Reports people send to admins are never filtered, so you always see what was reported.
