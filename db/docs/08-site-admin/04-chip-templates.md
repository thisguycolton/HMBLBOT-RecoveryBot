**Site Admin > Sobriety chips** sets the site's own chip design, and **Chip templates** holds the starting designs groups pick from when they design the chips their hosts give.

![Site Admin > Chip templates](/docs-images/site-admin/chip-templates.webp)

## Change a template

1. Open **Chip templates** and choose one.
2. Change its colors, metal or enamel finish, edge text and logo settings, and watch the chip update.
3. Save a draft, then publish it. History keeps every published version.

> [!NOTE] Earned chips stay generic: no group or meeting names on them.
