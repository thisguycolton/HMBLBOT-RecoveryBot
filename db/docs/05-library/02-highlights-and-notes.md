## Highlight

1. Select words while reading a chapter.
2. Choose a color in the bar that appears (on a phone, it docks at the bottom of the screen).
3. Add a **note** if you like. Notes take Markdown: **bold**, _italic_, lists and links.

## Find them again

**Highlights** in the sidebar lists every highlight and note you've made, newest first, by book.

## Who sees them

Each highlight has a setting: **Only me** (the default), **Followers** or **Members**. Change your default in Settings. Choosing **Share to my timeline** posts it to your followers' feed; that's always your choice.

## Reactions and reposts

People who can see your highlight can react to it (Like, Love, Hugs) and, if it's shared with Members, repost it to their own timeline.
