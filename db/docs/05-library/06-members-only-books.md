Some books in the Library are marked **Members only**. They're open to signed-in members of a group that lists the book in its approved literature.

## If a book is locked for you

The book still shows in the Library with a tag. Opening it lists the groups that read it, so you can [ask to join](/docs/group-tools/start-or-join-a-group) one.

## For group admins

Add the book to your group's **Approved literature** in [Group settings](/docs/group-tools/group-settings), and your members can read it.

> [!NOTE] Highlights from these books keep their quotes hidden from people who can't open the book, and can't be made into pictures.
