The **Library** (in the sidebar) holds the books: the Big Book, Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions, Daily Reflections and others.

![The Library](/docs-images/library/library.webp)

## Read

1. Open **Library** and choose a book.
2. Pick a chapter from its contents, or choose **Start reading**.
3. Move between chapters with the arrows at the top and bottom.

![A book's contents](/docs-images/library/book.webp)

Printed **page numbers** show in the margin, so you can follow along with someone reading from a paper copy.

![Reading Chapter 5, with a highlight](/docs-images/library/chapter.webp)

## Go to a page

On a book's page, type a page number in **Go to page** and choose **Go** to jump straight there. Step worksheets use the same trick: a reading like "page 63" or "Chapter 5" links right to it.

## Example

At the Sunrise Big Book Study, the group is reading from page 60. Jordan opens **Library > The Big Book of Alcoholics Anonymous**, types **60** in **Go to page**, and lands in Chapter 5, How It Works, right where the reader is.

## Search

The search box at the top finds chapters by name in every book, and the magnifying glass in the reader's bar searches inside the book you're reading.

## Comfortable reading

Use the theme button for dark mode. On a phone, the reader takes the whole screen; tap to bring the bar back.
