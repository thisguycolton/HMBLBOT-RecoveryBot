The **Library** (in the sidebar) holds the books: the Big Book, Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions, Daily Reflections and others.

## Read

1. Open **Library** and choose a book.
2. Pick a chapter from its contents.
3. Move between chapters with the arrows at the top and bottom.

Printed **page numbers** show in the margin, so you can follow along with someone reading from a paper copy.

## Go to a page

On a book's page, type a page number in **Go to page** to jump straight there. Step worksheets use the same trick: a reading like "page 63" or "Chapter 5" links right to it.

## Search

The search box at the top finds chapters by name in every book.

## Comfortable reading

Use the theme button for dark mode. On a phone, the reader takes the whole screen; tap to bring the bar back.
