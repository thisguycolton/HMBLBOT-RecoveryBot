A highlight can become a picture with the quote set in a nice frame, ready to post or text to a friend.

## Make one

1. Open a highlight (in the reader or on your Highlights page) and choose **Picture**.
2. Pick a **look**: Paper, Marker, Night or Sunrise.
3. Pick a **size**: Wide, Square or Story.
4. Choose whether to include your note and your first name (off by default).
5. **Share**, **Save**, **Copy picture** or **Copy link**. Or **Post to my timeline**.

A copied link shows the same picture as its preview in Messages and other apps.

> [!NOTE] Quotes from [members-only books](/docs/library/members-only-books) can't be made into pictures.
