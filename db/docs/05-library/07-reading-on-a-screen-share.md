When you share a reading in an online meeting, nobody needs to see your sidebar.

![A chapter opened for screen sharing, with no menus](/docs-images/library/screen-share.webp)

## From a HostDeck

Choose **Open to share** on a reading in the [Hosting Command Center](/docs/group-tools/hosting-command-center). It opens in its own window with no menus. Share that window in Zoom.

## From anywhere

Add `?screen=1` to the address of any chapter or reading. For example:

`/books/the-big-book-of-alcoholics-anonymous/chapters/chapter-5-how-it-works?screen=1`

A small card in the corner has **Back to HostDeck**, the theme button, and **Show menus** to go back to normal.

> [!TIP] Dark mode reads well on a shared screen at night.
