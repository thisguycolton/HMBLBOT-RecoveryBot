---
title: Reading on a screen share
summary: Open a reading or chapter without menus, for sharing your screen in a meeting.
icon: screen-share
---
When you share a reading in an online meeting, nobody needs to see your sidebar.

## From a HostDeck

Choose **Open to share** on a reading in the [Hosting Command Center](/docs/group-tools/hosting-command-center). It opens in its own window with no menus. Share that window in Zoom.

## From anywhere

Add `?screen=1` to the address of any chapter or reading. A small card in the corner has **Back to HostDeck**, the theme button, and **Show menus** to go back to normal.

> [!TIP] Dark mode reads well on a shared screen at night.
