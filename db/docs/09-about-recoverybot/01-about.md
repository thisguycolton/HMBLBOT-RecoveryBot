RecoveryBot is a free set of tools for people in twelve-step recovery and the groups that hold their meetings: finding a meeting, running one, reading the literature, working the steps, and keeping a home group organized.

## Who makes it

RecoveryBot is made by **Colton** under the name **HMBLBOT**, with help from friends in the fellowship who test things, suggest ideas and report what's broken. It began with one home group's online meetings and grew one request at a time.

## What we believe

- **Free to use.** No ads and no paid tiers for the tools members rely on.
- **Anonymity first.** Your recovery work is yours. We collect only what the tools need, never sell it, and explain all of it in the [privacy policy](/privacy).
- **Groups decide.** RecoveryBot keeps your group's records; your group conscience makes the decisions.
- **Open source.** The code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/thisguycolton/HMBLBOT-RecoveryBot).

## Not affiliated

RecoveryBot is an independent project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or speaking for Alcoholics Anonymous World Services, Inc., or any other fellowship. Literature in the Library is presented with care for its copyright; see the [terms of use](/terms).

## How it's paid for

Running the servers costs money. If RecoveryBot helps you or your group, **Help keep it running** at the bottom of any page takes donations.
