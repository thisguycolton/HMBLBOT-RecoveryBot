**Your story:** the home group, the problem you wanted to solve, and the first night it was used.

## 2023: a helper for the meeting

- **October 2023.** The first version goes live: meeting info and a page for the host, fixed for phones over the first few nights.
- **December 2023.** **The Topicificator** is finished: draw a topic, show it on screen, with readings and a dark screen for night meetings.

## 2024: the library

- **January 2024.** **Books and search** arrive, with chapters, a search results page and the first RecoveryBot branding.
- **July 2024.** Polls and editable topics.
- **October 2024.** Accounts, readings, an admin area, and the first idea of **groups**.
- **November 2024.** The **Host-burger Helper** gets an upgrade.

## 2025: more ways to meet

- **January 2025.** **Service Readings**: the Preamble, How It Works, the Steps and Traditions, ready to read aloud.
- **Spring 2025.** The **Hostificator** for host votes, email, a **Minecraft server** for the fellowship, and the first steps of a game.
- **July 2025.** **Topicificator 9002**.
- **August 2025.** The Library is rebuilt with dark mode.
- **November 2025.** AA Intergroup's online meetings come in, and meetings can be found by time zone.

## 2026: a home for whole groups

- **Early 2026.** The site is rebuilt piece by piece in React, page by page.
- **September 2026.** **The Acid Quest**, a pixel-art group journey through meeting topics, and readings that anyone can publish.
- **October 2026.** A month of building:
  - A new **Library** and reader, with the Twelve and Twelve and seven more books.
  - **Groups**: group pages, events, profiles and follows, and **Find a meeting** for everyone, no account needed.
  - **Group conscience**: documents, decisions by Robert's Rules, business meetings, elected officials and treasury.
  - **Hosts and subs**, the **Hosting Command Center**, then **HostDeck**.
  - A new look and navigation (the sidebar and "the stage"), Today, and the home-screen app with notifications.
  - **Chips**, the nightly **10th step**, **gratitude lists**, and **group chat** with mentions, replies, reactions, pins, pictures and files.
  - **Sponsorship** and **worksheets for all twelve steps**.
- **November 2026.** Step worksheets one page at a time, members-only books, live **Polls**, and these **docs**.
