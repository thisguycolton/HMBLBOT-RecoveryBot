**My page** (in the sidebar) is your profile: what other members see when they open your name.

![Sam's page](/docs-images/community/my-page.webp)

## What's on it

The header has your picture, name, username, **followers** and **following** counts and when you joined. Below it, by default: your **Timeline** (notes and shared highlights), **About** (your blurb and how long you've been sober, if you show it), **Groups** and **Following**.

## Lay it out

1. Open **My page** and choose **Edit your page**.
2. Add blocks: About, your timeline, your groups, people you follow, favorite chips, headings, notes, photos and galleries.
3. Drag them into order and choose **Save to my page**.

![Editing your page](/docs-images/community/page-editor.webp)

## Your picture and cover

Choose your picture or the cover at the top to change them. Your picture can also be changed in Settings (**Add a picture**).

## Someone else's page

On someone else's page you'll see **Follow** (or **Following**) and **•••** (block or report). Their timeline has a box to leave them a note: "Write something to Riley. They'll get a notification."

![Riley's page, as Sam sees it](/docs-images/community/someone-else.webp)

## Notes on your page

Friends can leave a note on your page (you can turn comments off on any note, or take a note off your page).

> [!PRIVACY] Profiles are only seen by signed-in members, never by the public web.
