## React

Under any note you can see, choose **Like**, **Love** or **Hugs**. Change it any time.

## Comment

Choose **Comment** to reply. Comments take Markdown and can have their own reactions. You can edit or delete your own.

## Repost

**Repost** puts someone's note on your timeline, linked to the original. Only notes shared with **Members** can be reposted, so a repost never shows a note to more people than its writer chose. If the writer narrows or deletes the note, reposts go away too.

## The ••• menu

On your own notes: **Edit**, **Turn comments off**, **Delete**. On a note on your page: **Take off my page**. On anyone's: **Report** or **Block**.

## Example

Under Jordan's note "92 days. Didn't think I'd see 9 days, let alone 90. Thank you, Lantern Street. #milestone", Sam chooses **Hugs** and comments "So glad you're here." Jordan gets a notification for each. Because Jordan shared the note with **Members**, Sam could also **Repost** it to Sam's own timeline, linked back to Jordan's.
