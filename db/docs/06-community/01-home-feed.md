Your **Home feed** is the page you land on after signing in.

![The home feed on a computer](/docs-images/community/home-feed.webp)

## What's on it

- **Notes** from you and the people you follow, newest first, and highlights they've shared.
- On the side (folded on phones): [Today](/docs/getting-started/find-your-way-around) with your day count, what needs you, your next meetings, sobriety birthdays in your groups, and events.
- **Find someone by name** searches people you can see.

## Write a note

1. Choose the box at the top ("What's on your mind today?"), or the robot button on a phone.
2. Write your note. Select words to format them, Markdown works, and you can add up to 6 pictures with **Photo**.
3. Pick who sees it (Only me, Followers, Members), and use **Also share to** to share it with one of your groups.
4. Choose **Post note**.

## Example

Sam follows Riley, Jordan and Quinn, so Sam's feed shows:

- Riley M. (5h ago): "Three things that helped me today: a phone call, a meeting, and a walk around the block. #gratitude"
- Jordan K. (1d ago): "92 days. Didn't think I'd see 9 days, let alone 90. Thank you, Lantern Street. #milestone"
- Sam's own shared highlights, with Sam's notes.

Under each one: **Like**, **Love**, **Hugs**, **Comment** and **Repost**.
