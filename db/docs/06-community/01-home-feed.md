Your **Home feed** is the page you land on after signing in.

## What's on it

- **Notes** from you and the people you follow, newest first.
- On the side (folded on phones): your next meetings, sobriety birthdays in your groups, and events.

## Write a note

1. Choose the box at the top, or the robot button on a phone.
2. Write your note. Markdown works, and you can add up to 6 pictures.
3. Pick who sees it (Only me, Followers, Members), and tick **Your groups** to also share it with a group.
4. Choose **Post**.

See [Timeline notes](/docs/community/timeline-notes) for reactions, comments and reposts.
