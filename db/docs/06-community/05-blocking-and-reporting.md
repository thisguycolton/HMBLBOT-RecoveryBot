## Block someone

Choose **•••** on any of their notes, comments or messages, or on their page, and choose **Block**.

- Their notes, reposts, comments and chat messages are hidden from you (chat messages fold away).
- They can't write on your page.
- You won't get notifications from them.
- **They aren't told.**

Group service (like who's hosting) still shows, so the group keeps running. Unblock from **Settings**.

## Report

Choose **•••** and **Report** to tell the site admins about a note, message or person. Say what's wrong in a few words. Reports go to the site admins only.

## Words that aren't allowed

Slurs are blocked when you post. If something you wrote is refused and you think it shouldn't be, [contact us](/contact).

> [!WARNING] If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number. RecoveryBot can't respond to emergencies.
