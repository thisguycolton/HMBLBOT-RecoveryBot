## Follow someone

![Following Riley from Riley's page](/docs-images/community/someone-else.webp)

Open their page and choose **Follow**. No approval needed; they get a notification that you followed them.

Their notes shared with **Followers** or **Members** now show in your home feed.

## Your lists

Your page shows **Followers** and **Following**. Choose either to see the list.

## Unfollow

Open their page and choose **Following** to stop. They aren't told.

## Example

Sam opens Riley M.'s page and sees **Following**: Sam already follows Riley, so Riley's notes shared with Followers or Members show in Sam's home feed, like "Three things that helped me today…". If Sam chose **Following** to stop, Riley wouldn't be told.
