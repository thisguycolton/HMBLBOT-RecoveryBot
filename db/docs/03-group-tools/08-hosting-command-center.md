The **Hosting Command Center** puts everything a host needs in one window: your group's script cut into sections, a timer that follows the meeting's start time, chat messages ready to paste into Zoom, and the readings.

![The Hosting Command Center for the Thursday meeting](/docs-images/group-tools/hostdeck.webp)

## Host a meeting

1. Open your group's **HostDeck** page and choose the meeting (or choose **HostDeck** next to it on the Meetings page, Hosting or Today when you're on the schedule).
2. Work through the script one section at a time with **Next**. **Meeting flow** on the left lists every section; sections that don't apply tonight are dimmed and skipped.
3. **Chat postings** pop up at their minute into the meeting. Choose **Copy** on one and paste it into the Zoom chat. Placeholders like `{{ zoom_passcode }}` are already filled in.
4. Open a **reading** to share it on screen. It opens without menus, ready for screen sharing.
5. **Milestones**: add each person celebrating by screen name and tick them off. There's confetti.

The co-host can open the same meeting; you both see the same section, timer and checklist live. **Hosts here** shows who has it open, and **Share with your co-host** gives a short link for tonight.

## Example: the Thursday script

The Lantern Street Group's script is a group document called **Thursday meeting format**. Its top headings become the sections hosts step through:

1. Before we begin
2. Readings
3. Introductions
4. Milestones
5. Discussion
6. 7th Tradition
7. Closing

Before the first section, the host fills in **People doing service today** (How It Works, The 12 Traditions, Timer, Closing reading). The script names them, so "How It Works: read by `{{ service_hiw }}`" reads out as the person's name. The host also picks **Today's reading / activity**: one of their readings, a reading from the literature, the Topicificator, a speaker, a meditation or something else.

Three chat postings are set for this meeting: **Welcome** at minute 0, **7th Tradition** at minute 45 and **Phone list** at minute 55.

## Setting it up (host coordinators)

Choose the gear to set up a meeting's **kit**: which group document is the script, the chat postings (title, icon, minute, text) and the readings. A group can have one default kit, with changes for single meetings.

### Start from a template

No script yet? Under **Script**, choose **Start from a template** and pick one, like the **Online meeting host format** (welcome, readings, newcomers and milestones, the day's activity, sharing and closing, with its sections, reminders and dynamic values already in place).

1. Give the group's copy a name and choose **Copy into the group's documents**. It goes in the **Format** folder, seen by hosts, and becomes the script.
2. Choose **Edit the document** and change the parts in parentheses, like your meeting times and announcements, to fit your group.
3. Save the kit.

The copy is your group's own: changing it doesn't change the template, and later changes to the template don't change it.

> [!NOTE] The Command Center is the built-in **Host a meeting** layout of a [HostDeck](/docs/group-tools/hostdeck), so you can rearrange it too.
