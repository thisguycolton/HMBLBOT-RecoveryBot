**Decisions** is your group's record of every motion and what came of it.

![The Decisions tab](/docs-images/group-tools/decisions.webp)

## Record a decision (record keepers)

1. Open **Conscience > Decisions** and choose **Record a motion**.
2. Pick the **kind of motion**: a main motion, an amendment, to table, to refer, to reconsider…
3. Fill in who moved and seconded it, how it was voted, and the counts.
4. Link it to the business meeting and agenda item it came from.

Motions that apply to another (like an amendment) are kept with the motion they change, so you can follow the whole thread.

## Example: the Big Book motion

At the November business meeting, Alex P. moved "That the group buy 20 copies of Alcoholics Anonymous to give to newcomers, spending up to $250." Quinn A. seconded. Sam recorded it as:

- **Title**: Buy 20 Big Books for newcomers
- **Category**: Money
- **Vote**: 10 for, 0 against, 1 abstained. **Passed**
- **Made at**: the business meeting on Sunday, November 1, 2026

Choosing it opens it in the pane on the right, with **Take a poll on this**, **Back**, **Delete** and **Change**, and **Might be related**: other decisions that use the same words.

![A decision opened in the side pane](/docs-images/group-tools/decision.webp)

## Help with Robert's Rules

As you type, RecoveryBot points out where a record doesn't follow the rules: no second, a count that doesn't carry, a two-thirds vote taken the wrong way. These are hints for record keepers, never blocks. **Robert's Rules at a glance** on the Meetings tab sums up what each motion needs and what outranks what.

## Standing decisions

The **Standing** list shows what's currently in force, grouped by category (Money, Format, Service, Literature, Meeting, Other). Switch to **All decisions**, **Failed**, **Unfinished** or **Procedure**, filter by kind and date, or search by words. A decision that replaced another links to it.

At Lantern Street, three decisions are standing: the Big Book purchase, "Keep 3 months of rent as prudent reserve" and "Hold a gratitude potluck in November". A fourth, "Add a 7th Tradition QR code to the script", was tabled, so it shows under **Unfinished**.

## Taking a poll

**Take a poll on this** makes a [poll](/docs/meetings/polls) for members. When it closes, **Use these counts** fills in the vote.

## Moving your records in

**Import JSON** and **Download JSON** move a group's decisions in and out, for example from a spreadsheet of old minutes. Imports show a preview first.
