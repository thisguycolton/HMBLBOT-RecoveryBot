**Decisions** is your group's record of every motion and what came of it.

## Record a decision (record keepers)

1. Open **Conscience > Decisions** and choose **Record a decision**.
2. Pick the **kind of motion**: a main motion, an amendment, to table, to refer, to reconsider…
3. Fill in who moved and seconded it, how it was voted, and the counts.
4. Link it to the business meeting and agenda item it came from.

Motions that apply to another (like an amendment) are kept with the motion they change, so you can follow the whole thread.

## Help with Robert's Rules

As you type, RecoveryBot points out where a record doesn't follow the rules: no second, a count that doesn't carry, a two-thirds vote taken the wrong way. These are hints for record keepers, never blocks.

## Standing decisions

The Decisions pane lists what's currently in force, and what replaced what. Search by words or by date.

## Taking a poll

**Take a poll on this** makes a [poll](/docs/meetings/polls) for members. When it closes, **Use these counts** fills in the vote.
