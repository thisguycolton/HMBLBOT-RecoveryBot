The **Members** page lists everyone in the group. Group admins also see requests to join and the invite link.

![The Members page for a group admin](/docs-images/group-tools/members.webp)

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Everything: settings, members, meetings, the group page, moving chat messages. |
| **Record keeper** | Business meetings, minutes, decisions, documents marked for record keepers. |
| **Host coordinator** | The host schedule: assign hosts, fill gaps, approve swaps. Can be limited to some time slots. |
| **Treasurer** | Monthly treasury reports. |
| **Host** (**Can host**) | Hosting meetings: the Hosting Command Center and giving chips. |
| **Member** | Chat, events, the group's members-only documents and pages. |

A person can hold several roles; each one shows as a tag next to their name. Each role adds pages to that group's [dashboard](/docs/group-tools/group-dashboard).

## Example: who does what at Lantern Street

- **Sam R.**: group admin, record keeper, host coordinator, can host.
- **Riley M.**: group admin, record keeper, can host (and the group's secretary).
- **Morgan T.**: host coordinator, can host.
- **Alex P.**: treasurer, can host.

## Bring people in (admins)

- **Invite link**: anyone who opens it joins the group, after making an account if they need one. **Copy** it to send, or **New link** to turn off the old one.
- **Add someone who has an account, by email**, and choose **Add**.

## Approve requests (admins)

New requests show at the top of Members with a count in the sidebar. Choose **Approve** or **Decline**.

## Change someone's roles (admins)

Open the member, tick the roles they hold, and save. They'll see the new pages the next time they open the group.

## Sponsors in this group

If members have said they're available to sponsor, the Members page lists them with a green check. See [Sponsorship](/docs/step-work/sponsorship).
