**Elected officials** lists your group's service positions (secretary, treasurer, GSR, intergroup rep…) and who holds each one.

![Elected officials](/docs-images/group-tools/officials.webp)

## Set up positions (record keepers)

Choose **Add an office** and give it a **term length** and any **sobriety minimum** your group asks for. Each office card has **Change**, **Retire** and delete buttons.

## Record a term

Choose **Next person** (or **Start a term**), enter the person's name (and link their account if they have one) and the start date. Starting a new term ends the one before it.

## Example: Lantern Street's officers

| Office | Term | Sobriety asked | Held by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Secretary | 12 months | 6 months | Riley M. |
| Treasurer | 12 months | 6 months | Alex P. |
| Host coordinator | 6 months | 6 months | Morgan T. |
| GSR | 24 months | 6 months | Quinn A. |

Morgan's 6-month term as host coordinator ends first. A month before it does, if nobody is lined up as **Next person**, Morgan and the record keepers get a reminder.

## Reminders

When a term is 30 days or less from ending and nobody is lined up after it, record keepers (and the person holding it) get a reminder, once per term.
