**Elected officials** lists your group's service positions (secretary, treasurer, GSR, intergroup rep…) and who holds each one.

![Elected officials](/docs-images/group-tools/officials.webp)

## Set up positions (record keepers)

Choose **Add an office** and give it a **term length** and any **sobriety minimum** your group asks for. Each office card has **Change**, **Retire** and delete buttons.

## What an office can do (admins)

In **Manage > Offices**, group admins choose **What it can do** for each office:

- **Nothing extra** (a GSR or chair, say).
- **Record keeper**: the agenda, minutes and documents.
- **Treasurer**: the treasurer's reports.
- **Host coordinator**: the host schedule, for **the whole group** or **only some time slots** (pick the times, and the days of each).

Whoever holds the office can do that, starting the day their term starts. When the term ends or passes to the next person, so does the permission (unless another office of theirs gives it too). The holder needs to be linked to their account. Group admins can do everything, and show at the top as **Group admin: Everything**.

To add one fast, **Start from a common office**: Secretary, Treasurer, Host Coordinator, GSR, Chair or a blank office. Each fills in the title, what it involves, a usual term and what it can do, and you can change anything before saving.

## Record a term

Choose **Next person** (or **Start a term**), enter the person's name (and link their account if they have one) and the start date. Starting a new term ends the one before it.

## Example: Lantern Street's officers

| Office | Term | Sobriety asked | Held by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Secretary | 12 months | 6 months | Riley M. |
| Treasurer | 12 months | 6 months | Alex P. |
| Host coordinator (9 AM and noon) | 6 months | 6 months | Morgan T. |
| GSR | 24 months | 6 months | Quinn A. |

Riley is a record keeper as Secretary, Alex writes the treasury reports as Treasurer, and Morgan schedules the hosts of the 9 AM and noon meetings. Morgan's 6-month term ends first. A month before it does, if nobody is lined up as **Next person**, Morgan and the record keepers get a reminder.

## Reminders

When a term is 30 days or less from ending and nobody is lined up after it, record keepers (and the person holding it) get a reminder, once per term.
