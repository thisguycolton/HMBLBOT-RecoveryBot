Group admins find **Settings** in the group's folder in the sidebar.

## What you can change

- **About the group**: name, description, location, logo and cover picture.
- **Meetings**: add, edit or remove meetings. Link a meeting to its AA Intergroup listing to keep it in sync.
- **Invite link**: copy it to invite members straight in. **Make a new link** turns off the old one.
- **Privacy**: whether host names show on the public page, and whether members available to sponsor are listed.
- **HostDeck**: who can rearrange decks.
- **Chat**: who can add channels.
- **Approved literature**: the books your group reads, which also unlocks [members-only books](/docs/library/members-only-books) for your members.

## Blocked people

Admins can block someone from the group. They can't ask to join again, and their chat messages are hidden.
