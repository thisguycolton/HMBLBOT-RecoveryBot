Every group has its own **Chat**, with channels like #general and #hosts.

## Chatting

1. Open your group's **Chat** from the sidebar.
2. Pick a channel and write in the box at the bottom. Enter sends; Shift+Enter starts a new line.
3. Add pictures or files with the paperclip (up to 6 at a time), or attach one of the group's documents.

Links show a preview card. You can hide the card on your own messages.

## Mentions

Type **@** to mention someone. You can also mention a role (like @hosts or @record-keepers) or a service position by its title. Admins can use **@everyone**. People mentioned get a notification.

## Replies, reactions and pins

- **Reply** quotes the message you're answering, and its writer is notified.
- **React** with an emoji: long-press on a phone, or hover on a computer.
- **Pin** keeps important messages a tap away (admins and whoever made the channel). The pin button in the channel's header lists them.

## Channels

Admins decide who can add channels (by role or by person) in the chat settings. Drag channels to reorder them. Admins can also **move** a message, with its replies, to a better channel.

> [!PRIVACY] Chat is for the group's members only. Files are only reachable by people who can see the channel.

If someone is bothering you, see [Blocking and reporting](/docs/community/blocking-and-reporting).
