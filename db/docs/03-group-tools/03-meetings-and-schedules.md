The **Meetings** page of a group opens on what's next: each meeting with its time, Join link and passcode, and who's hosting.

![A group's Meetings page: next up and the week](/docs-images/group-tools/meetings.webp)

## Next up and the week

- **Next up** shows the next meeting with its countdown, host and co-host, a **HostDeck** button for hosts, the passcode and **Join**.
- **Week by week** shows every meeting date with its host and co-host. Use ‹ and › to move between weeks; today's meetings are marked **Today**.
- **Your dates** lists the dates you're hosting, with a one-tap **I can't make it** that asks for a sub.

![Your dates and the week view](/docs-images/group-tools/meetings-week.webp)

## Example: the Lantern Street week

The Lantern Street Group holds three weekly meetings:

| Meeting | When | Where | Host / co-host |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sunrise Big Book Study | Mondays, 6:30–7:30 AM | Online | Jordan K. / Quinn A. |
| Thursday Night Step Meeting | Thursdays, 7–8 PM | Online | Morgan T. / Jamie W. |
| Saturday Speaker Meeting | Saturdays, 10–11 AM | Riverbend Community Center, Room 4 | Riley M. / Alex P. |

This Saturday Sam is covering for Alex as co-host, so Sam's **Your dates** shows "Sat, Nov 14 · Co-host" with **I can't make it** under it.

## Times and time zones

A group keeps its meetings in its own time zone (Lantern Street uses Arizona time); everyone sees them in theirs.

## Changing meetings

Group admins add, edit and remove meetings in [Group settings](/docs/group-tools/group-settings) under **Meeting settings**. A meeting held on several days is one **series**: change it once and every day follows.

See [Hosts and subs](/docs/group-tools/hosts-and-subs) for the host schedule itself.
