The **Meetings** page of a group opens on today: each meeting with its time, Join link and passcode, and who's hosting.

## Today and the week

- **Today's cards** show each meeting with its host and co-host.
- Below, the **week view** shows every meeting date with its hosts. Use ‹ and › to move between weeks.
- **Your dates** lists the dates you're hosting, with a one-tap **I can't make it** that asks for a sub.

## Times and time zones

A group keeps its meetings in its own time zone; everyone sees them in theirs.

## Changing meetings

Group admins add, edit and remove meetings in [Group settings](/docs/group-tools/group-settings) under **Meetings**. A meeting held on several days is one **series**: change it once and every day follows.

See [Hosts and subs](/docs/group-tools/hosts-and-subs) for the host schedule itself.
