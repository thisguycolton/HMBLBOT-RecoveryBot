**Events** are for anything beyond the regular meetings: workshops, picnics, speaker nights, anniversaries.

![A group's Events tab](/docs-images/group-tools/events.webp)

## Make an event (group admins)

1. Open your group's **Events**, type a title under **Start an event**, and choose **New event**.
2. The event opens in the **HMBLBOT Editor**. Add the date and place, then blocks: notes, an agenda, guidelines, a poll, photos or a gallery.
3. Add **collaborators** (other group members) to help edit it.
4. **Publish** it when it's ready. Until then only editors see it.

## Example: the gratitude potluck

Sam makes **Lantern Street Gratitude Potluck**: "Bring a dish, bring a friend. Speaker at 7, food at 6." It's on a Thursday evening from 6 to 9 PM at the Riverbend Community Center. Once it's published, it shows under **Upcoming events** on the group's Events tab and in **Coming up** on the group page, with a count of who's going.

![The event's own page](/docs-images/group-tools/event.webp)

## RSVPs

Published events have their own link anyone can open. People answer **Are you coming?** with **Going**, **Maybe** or **Can't go**; guests just give a name and email. Editors see "You can edit this event" and **Open in the editor** at the top.

## Updates

Mark a notes or guidelines block as an **update** and everyone who RSVP'd hears about changes by notification and email. Guests can unsubscribe from the email.

## Finding a time

Not sure when to hold it? A schedule poll lets people mark the times they can make, or a quick [poll](/docs/meetings/polls) can pick between a few dates.
