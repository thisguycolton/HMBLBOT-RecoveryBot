**Events** are for anything beyond the regular meetings: workshops, picnics, speaker nights, anniversaries.

## Make an event (group admins)

1. Open your group's **Events** and choose **New event**.
2. The event opens in the **HMBLBOT Editor**. Add a title, date and place, then blocks: notes, an agenda, guidelines, a poll, photos or a gallery.
3. Add **collaborators** (other group members) to help edit it.
4. **Publish** it when it's ready. Until then only editors see it.

## RSVPs

Published events have their own link anyone can open. People RSVP **Going**, **Maybe** or follow it; guests just give a name and email.

## Updates

Mark a notes or guidelines block as an **update** and everyone who RSVP'd hears about changes by notification and email. Guests can unsubscribe from the email.

## Finding a time

Not sure when to hold it? A schedule poll lets people mark the times they can make.
