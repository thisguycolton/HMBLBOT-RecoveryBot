## Plan a business meeting (record keepers)

1. Open **Conscience > Meetings** and choose **New business meeting**.
2. Set the date and time, and link it to one of the group's meetings or an event if you like.
3. Members are notified that it's scheduled.

## The agenda

- Members **suggest items**. Record keepers get a notification and approve, reorder or cross them off.
- Recording a decision on an agenda item settles that item.

## Minutes

1. Open the meeting and write the minutes in the editor. They're saved as a **draft** only record keepers see.
2. Choose **Post** to share them with members (members are notified the first time).
3. Once the group approves them, mark them **Approved**.

## Print

Choose **Print** for a clean copy of the agenda or minutes.
