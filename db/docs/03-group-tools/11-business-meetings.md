## Plan a business meeting (record keepers)

1. Open **Conscience > Meetings** and choose **Schedule a meeting**.
2. Set the date and time, and link it to one of the group's meetings or an event if you like ("After Saturday Speaker Meeting").
3. Members are notified that it's scheduled.

Set the group's **Quorum** (what the bylaws ask for) at the top of the page, so the record shows whether a vote had enough people. **Import old minutes** brings in past minutes from files.

## The agenda

- Members **suggest items** from the meeting or the Conscience overview: a title and a kind (report, old business, new business). Record keepers get a notification and approve, reorder or cross them off.
- Recording a decision on an agenda item settles that item.

## Minutes

1. Open the meeting and write the minutes in the editor on the right. They're saved as a **draft** only record keepers see.
2. Choose **Post** to share them with members (members are notified the first time).
3. Once the group approves them, mark them **Approved**.

## Print

Choose **Print** for a clean copy of the agenda or minutes.
