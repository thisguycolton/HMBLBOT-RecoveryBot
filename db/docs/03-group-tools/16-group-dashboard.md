If you hold a role in a group, its **Dashboard** collects what needs you, one box per role:

![A group dashboard for someone with every role](/docs-images/group-tools/dashboard.webp)

- **Group admin**: join requests and settings that need attention, with links to Members, Settings, Meeting settings and the group page.
- **Record keeper**: suggested agenda items, minutes waiting to be posted, draft documents and terms ending.
- **Host coordinator**: open sub requests, gaps in the schedule, who's covering for whom.
- **Treasurer**: whether last month's report is in.

Along the top: members, standing decisions, business meetings held and the last treasurer's report.

## Example: Sam's dashboard

Sam is an admin, record keeper and host coordinator at Lantern Street, so the dashboard shows:

- **Group admin**: "Nobody is waiting to join."
- **Record keeper**: "Suggested for Dec 6, 2026: Start a newcomer welcome table" (put it on the agenda or decline it) and "Bylaws (draft)".
- **Host coordinator**: "You're filling in for Alex P. as co-host for the 10 AM Saturday Speaker meeting" and "Thursday Night Step Meeting, Nov 26, 2026: needs a host".
- **Treasurer**: "No reports in draft."

The same items show on Today for the week ahead. Clear an item once it's handled; it comes back if it still needs you.
