title: Your group dashboard
summary: What needs you in each role you hold, in one place.
icon: layout-dashboard
---
If you hold a role in a group, its **Dashboard** collects what needs you:

- **Admins**: join requests and settings that need attention.
- **Record keepers**: suggested agenda items, minutes waiting to be posted, terms ending.
- **Host coordinators**: open sub requests, gaps in the schedule, who's covering for whom.
- **Treasurers**: whether last month's report is in.

The same items show on Today for the week ahead. Clear an item once it's handled; it comes back if it still needs you.
