A group's **Home** is what newcomers see first: when you meet, how to join, and whatever else your group wants to share. Group admins lay it out in the **HMBLBOT Editor**.

![The Lantern Street Group's home page, as a member sees it](/docs-images/group-tools/group-page.webp)

## What's on it by default

- The group's name, description, time zone, member count and website.
- **Next meeting** with a countdown ("in 1 day 11h"), a **Join** button and the meeting passcode with a copy button.
- Tabs for **Home**, **Meetings**, **Events**, **Readings**, **Literature**, **Group conscience** and **Members**.
- Every meeting the group holds, with a map for groups that meet in person.
- A **Join us** card, upcoming events (**Coming up**) and the group's timeline.

Members also see buttons for what they can do: **Dashboard**, **Edit page** and **Settings** for admins, **Leave** and **Share** for everyone.

## What a newcomer sees

Signed out, the same page shows **Ask to join** instead of the member buttons, and leaves out anything just for members:

![The same page, signed out](/docs-images/group-tools/group-page-guest.webp)

On a phone, the header stacks and the tabs scroll sideways:

![The group page on a phone](/docs-images/group-tools/group-page-phone.webp)

## Edit the page (group admins)

1. Open the group's **Home** and choose **Edit page** (or **Edit the page** on the "This is the default group page" note).
2. Add blocks from the list on the left: headings, notes, pictures, galleries, events, meetings, timeline, members, literature and more.
3. Drag blocks to reorder them. Pick a **size** for each (compact, auto or full) and a **color** from the palette. On a phone, use the arrows to move a block up or down.
4. Choose **Save**. Changes show right away.

![The HMBLBOT Editor for a group page](/docs-images/group-tools/page-editor.webp)

> [!TIP] **Reset to default** brings back the standard layout if you get lost.

## Who sees it

Group pages are public, so people searching for a meeting can find yours. Member-only things (chat, members, records) never appear on it.
