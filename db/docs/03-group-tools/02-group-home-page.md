A group's **Home** is what newcomers see first: when you meet, how to join, and whatever else your group wants to share. Group admins lay it out in the **HMBLBOT Editor**.

## What's on it by default

- The group's name, logo and cover picture.
- **Next meeting** with a countdown and Join button, and the meeting passcode.
- Every meeting the group holds.
- A map, for groups that meet in person.
- Upcoming events and the group's timeline.

## Edit the page (group admins)

1. Open the group's **Home** and choose **Edit page**.
2. Add blocks with **Add block**: headings, notes, pictures, galleries, events, meetings, timeline and more.
3. Drag blocks to reorder them. Pick a **size** for each (compact, auto or full) and a **color** from the palette.
4. Choose **Save**. Changes show right away.

> [!TIP] **Reset to default** brings back the standard layout if you get lost.

## Who sees it

Group pages are public, so people searching for a meeting can find yours. Member-only things (chat, members, records) never appear on it.
