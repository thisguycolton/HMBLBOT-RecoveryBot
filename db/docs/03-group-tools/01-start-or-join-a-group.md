A **group** on RecoveryBot is your home group's space: its meetings, members, chat, events and records.

## Join a group

1. Find it: search for it in the top bar, open **Find your group** (from Find a meeting, or the **Find a group** link in Settings), or open a meeting in [Find a meeting](/docs/meetings/find-a-meeting) and choose its group.
2. On the group's page, choose **Ask to join**. Not signed in? You'll make an account first and come right back.
3. A group admin approves you, and the group appears in your sidebar under **Your groups**.

**Find your group** asks which meeting you go to. Search by name, paste a Zoom link or meeting ID, or pick a day and time:

![Find your group](/docs-images/group-tools/find-your-group.webp)

Got an **invite link** from someone in the group? Open it and you're in, no waiting.

## Example

Casey goes to the Thursday Night Step Meeting on Zoom. Casey opens **Find your group**, pastes the Zoom link from the meeting invite, and the Lantern Street Group comes up. Casey chooses **Ask to join**; Riley (a group admin) approves it from the Members page, and Lantern Street Group appears in Casey's sidebar with its Chat, Events and Members.

## Start a group

If your group meets online and is listed by AA Intergroup, it may already have a page waiting for it.

1. Search for your group, or go to **Find a meeting** and open one of its meetings.
2. Choose **Start this group on RecoveryBot** (or **Start a group** if it isn't listed).
3. Match up the meetings it holds. Listed meetings stay in sync with AA Intergroup.
4. You're the group's first **admin**. Invite your members with the invite link from the [Members page](/docs/group-tools/members-and-roles).

> [!NOTE] Groups are being opened up gradually. If you don't see how to start one yet, [contact us](/contact) and we'll set it up with you.

## Leave a group

Open the group's page and choose **Leave**, or find yourself on its **Members** page and choose **Leave group**.
