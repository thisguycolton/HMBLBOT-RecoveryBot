Every weekly meeting has a **host** and a **co-host** spot. The host schedule lives on your group's [Meetings](/docs/group-tools/meetings-and-schedules) page.

## If you're a host

- **Your dates** lists the meetings you're hosting soon.
- Can't make one? Choose **I can't make it**. That posts a **Need a sub** request, and people who can host see it in their Hosting badge and Today.
- You can get a reminder before each meeting you host (24 hours before by default). Turn it on in **Settings > Hosting**.

## Covering for someone

Open **Hosting** in the sidebar (or Today) to see open sub requests you could take, and choose **I'll cover it**. The original host and the host coordinators are told.

![The Hosting page: a sub needed, and your next two weeks](/docs-images/group-tools/hosting.webp)

## Example: Morgan's trip

Morgan T. hosts the Thursday Night Step Meeting, but will be traveling on Nov 26.

1. On the group's Meetings page, Morgan chooses **I can't make it** on that date and adds a note: "Traveling for work."
2. Everyone the group lets see sub requests (here, the people who can host) now sees **Subs needed** on their Hosting page, with a **1** next to Hosting in the sidebar. Sam (a host coordinator) also sees "No host yet" on Today.
3. Jamie chooses **I'll cover it**. Morgan and the host coordinators are told, and the week view shows Jamie for that date only.

Below the sub requests, **Your next two weeks** lists your own dates. Sam's shows the Saturday Speaker Meeting as **Co-host, Covering**, with a **HostDeck** button.

## Swaps and permanent changes (host coordinators)

- **Swap** in the week view, or **Cover a date** under Regular hosts, offers **Just this date** or **Make it permanent**. Permanent changes start on that date; earlier dates keep whoever had them.
- **Gaps only** in the week view shows only the empty spots; tap one to fill it.
- Under **Regular hosts**, choose **Change** to set who hosts a meeting from a date onward.

> [!TIP] A host coordinator office can cover just some time slots (say, the morning meetings). Group admins pick them in Manage > Offices.

## Who sees sub requests

Group admins choose this in [Manage](/docs/group-tools/group-settings) under **Meetings setup**:

- **All members** (the default): everyone in the group sees "A sub is needed", hears about it and can take it.
- **Only people who can host**: just the members marked **Can host**, plus host coordinators, record keepers and admins.
- **Off**: only the host coordinators see requests and fill the place.

Host coordinators always see the requests for their own time slots, and whoever asked always sees their own.

## Showing hosts publicly

Groups can show host names on their public page, or keep them to members. Admins set this in [Manage](/docs/group-tools/group-settings).
