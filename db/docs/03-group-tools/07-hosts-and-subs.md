Every weekly meeting has a **host** and a **co-host** spot. The host schedule lives on your group's [Meetings](/docs/group-tools/meetings-and-schedules) page.

## If you're a host

- **Your dates** lists the meetings you're hosting soon.
- Can't make one? Choose **I can't make it**. That posts a **Need a sub** request, and people who can host see it in their Hosting badge and Today.
- You can get a reminder before each meeting you host (24 hours before by default). Turn it on in your hosting settings.

## Covering for someone

Open **Hosting** in the sidebar (or Today) to see open sub requests you could take, and choose **I'll cover it**. The original host and the host coordinators are told.

## Swaps and permanent changes (host coordinators)

- **Swap** in the week view, or **Cover a date** under Regular hosts, offers **Just this date** or **Make it permanent**. Permanent changes start on that date; earlier dates keep whoever had them.
- **Gaps only** in the week view shows only the empty spots; tap one to fill it.
- Under **Regular hosts**, choose **Change** to set who hosts a meeting from a date onward.

> [!TIP] Host coordinators can be limited to some time slots (say, the morning meetings) in Members.

## Showing hosts publicly

Groups can show host names on their public page, or keep them to members. Admins set this in [Group settings](/docs/group-tools/group-settings).
