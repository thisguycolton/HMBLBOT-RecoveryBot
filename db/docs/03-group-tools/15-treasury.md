**Treasury** keeps one report per month.

![The Treasury tab](/docs-images/group-tools/treasury.webp)

## Write a report (treasurers)

1. Open **Conscience > Treasury** and choose **New report**, then the month.
2. Enter the opening balance, the money in (**Income**) and out (**Expenses**), and your **prudent reserve**.
3. Set how anything above the reserve is shared (for example, between district, area and the General Service Office). RecoveryBot does the sums in the panel on the right.
4. Choose who can see it, save it as a draft, then **Post** it for members.

![October's report, worked out](/docs-images/group-tools/treasury-report.webp)

## Example: October at Lantern Street

Alex P., the treasurer, entered:

| | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Opening balance | $680.00 |
| 7th Tradition (basket) | +$312.50 |
| 7th Tradition (online) | +$100.00 |
| Rent, community center | −$180.00 |
| Literature | −$64.00 |
| Coffee and supplies | −$23.50 |
| **Closing balance** | **$825.00** |
| Prudent reserve to keep | $540.00 |
| **Above the reserve** | **$285.00** |

The group shares what's above the reserve 30% to the district, 10% to the area and 60% to the General Service Office, so RecoveryBot works out $85.50, $28.50 and $171.00. Alex's note says the 20 Big Books for newcomers were bought, as the group decided.

Members read posted reports. Treasurers keep drafts to themselves until they're ready. **Print** makes a clean copy for the business meeting.

> [!TIP] If last month has no report yet, the treasurer sees a reminder on Today.
