**Treasury** keeps one report per month.

## Write a report (treasurers)

1. Open **Conscience > Treasury** and choose the month.
2. Enter the opening balance, the money in and out, and your **prudent reserve**.
3. Set how anything above the reserve is shared (for example, between district, area and the General Service Office). RecoveryBot does the sums.
4. Save it as a draft, then **Post** it for members.

Members read posted reports. Treasurers keep drafts to themselves until they're ready.

> [!TIP] If last month has no report yet, the treasurer sees a reminder on Today.
